Importance of Digital Marketing for Healthcare

Did you know? Before booking an appointment, 70 to 82% of patients search online for healthcare providers. Healthcare has changed with the growth of the internet. People now use search engines, websites, social media and online reviews to find health-related information and healthcare providers. This makes digital marketing for healthcare an important part of how clinics, hospitals and other healthcare organisations reach people.
In this guide, we will explain the importance of digital marketing in healthcare and its role.  

Key Takeaways

  • Digital marketing helps healthcare providers reach patients online through search engines, social media, email and other digital channels.
  • A strong online presence can attract more patients and build trust by making healthcare services easier to find.
  • Useful and accurate content helps establish healthcare authority.
  • A patient-focused digital journey improves the experience by helping people book appointments more easily.
  • The NHS App campaign shows the value of targeted digital marketing, reaching more than 355,000 in just four weeks.

What Is Digital Marketing for Healthcare?

Digital marketing for healthcare means using online channels to promote health-related services, share useful information and help people connect with healthcare providers. These channels can include:
  • Search engines
  • Social media
  • Email
  • Online reviews
  • Local search listings

‘Digital technologies can play a powerful role in improving the health of people worldwide.’

— Dr Bernardo Mariano, WHO Director of Digital Health and Innovation.

Effective healthcare digital marketing should do more than promote a service. It helps people find clear, useful and trustworthy information. For example, people may search for information about a health service. They may then visit a website, read about the service, check the location and book an appointment.

Benefits of Digital Marketing for Healthcare

Digital marketing for healthcare can help providers reach people when they are looking for health services. It can also help providers attract new patients and make their services easier to access. Below, we explain some points that help you understand the importance of digital marketing for healthcare.

1. Attract More Patients

Online marketing helps clinics to reach people in specific regions and appear when people search for any health-related query. For example, a local clinic may want to appear for searches such as:
  • GP near me
  • Dentist in Manchester
  • Private cardiologist in London
  • Dermatologist near me

2. Reach Your Target Audience

Not everyone needs the same service. Different groups of people may have different needs. Online channels allow hospitals to create content for specific audiences. For example, a dental practice can create separate content for:
  • Dental care
  • Children’s dentistry
  • Teeth cleaning
  • Dental implants
  • Cosmetic dentistry

3. Establish a Healthcare Authority

Trust is very important in healthcare. Patients need to trust your expertise and skills before choosing your services. One good way to build this trust is by sharing useful online content that answers their questions and solves common issues. For example, you can create blog posts or YouTube videos about common health concerns your patients face. This can help build a stronger connection with your audience. To build authority, you can share:
  • Doctor profiles
  • Treatment guides
  • Health articles
  • FAQs
  • Educational videos
  • References to reliable medical research
  • Regular reviews and updates to medical content

4. Improve Patient Communication

Digital channels give patients more ways to contact clinics with ease. Having a website and social media profiles makes this process easy. By engaging with people on these platforms, you can reach more potential patients and build stronger connections with them. This can give you more access to digital platforms and lets patients contact you through the channels they prefer.

5. Strengthen Your Online Presence

Your website, search listings, social media profiles and online reviews all affect how people see your clinic. A strong online presence should provide basic information such as:
  • Services
  • Location
  • Opening hours
  • Contact details
  • Appointment options
So, you should keep this information up to date. An outdated phone number or incorrect opening time can create frustration, while a clear and useful website can make it easier for people to take the next step.

6. Increase Brand Awareness

Brand awareness means people recognise and remember your clinic. Consistent content, clear messaging and a strong online presence can help build this recognition. A strong brand can help your healthcare practice stand out in a competitive market. A good online presence keeps your practice in people’s minds when they need a healthcare service. This can make them more likely to contact you or book an appointment.

7. Protect Your Professional Reputation

Digital marketing for healthcare can help you build a strong online reputation. A review management system can encourage patients to share their reviews. It also helps you respond to complaints and deal with fake or misleading reviews. Remember that you should never share a patient’s private details in a public response. The best practice is that if a patient raises a personal health issue online, move the conversation to a suitable private channel.

8. Improve the Patient Experience

A mobile-friendly website, simple navigation, clear calls to action and accessible content can all help to improve the patient experience. The aim of digital marketing for healthcare should be to remove unnecessary steps, not add more. In addition, a good online journey should make simple tasks easier. Patients should be able to:
  1. Find the right information.
  2. Understand the service.
  3. Find contact details.
  4. Ask questions.
  5. Book or request care.

Case Study: How Digital Marketing Strategy for Healthcare Helped the Clinic Reach Target Audience

The NHS provides a strong UK example of how digital marketing can improve access to healthcare services. In 2024, a campaign in Northwest London used paid ads on Facebook and Instagram to promote the NHS app. The campaign ran for four weeks and reached a targeted audience aged 18 to 64.
After four weeks, the campaign reached more than 355,000 people and generated 8,617 clicks to the NHS app. It also achieved a 2.4% click-through rate, which was above industry benchmarks reported by the campaign team. The campaign also used translated posters and multilingual business cards to support people from different language groups. This helped make the campaign more accessible to local communities.
This case shows the value of digital marketing for healthcare when the right message is shown to the right audience. The key lesson for healthcare providers is simple: know your audience, use the right digital channel and make the next step clear.

Conclusion

Digital marketing for healthcare is about much more than getting clicks or views. It helps healthcare providers reach patients, share helpful information, build trust and make their services easier to find. A good digital marketing strategy should focus on the patient’s journey. Make it easy for people to find the information they need, understand their options, contact a healthcare provider and take the next step.
