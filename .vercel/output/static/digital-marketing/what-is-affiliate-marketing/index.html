What Is Affiliate Marketing? A Beginner’s Guide to Getting Started

If you’re considering becoming an affiliate marketer, now is a good time to explore the opportunity. The global affiliate marketing industry was valued at $18.5 billion in 2024 and is expected to reach $31.7 billion by 2031. What’s more, 81% of brands use affiliate marketing programmes to promote their products or services. But how does affiliate marketing work, and how can you get started?
In simple terms, affiliate marketers recommend products or services to their audience and earn a commission when those recommendations lead to a qualifying action, such as a purchase. With the right niche, content and audience, affiliate marketing can become a source of income.
This guide explains what affiliate marketing is, how it works and how affiliate marketers get paid. It will also walk you through the steps you can follow to start your journey as an affiliate marketer.

Key Takeaways

  • Affiliate marketing allows you to earn a commission by promoting another business’s products or services.
  • The process involves an affiliate, a business, a customer and, in some cases, an affiliate network.
  • Affiliate links help businesses track referrals and attribute qualifying actions to the right affiliate.
  • Beginners can start by choosing a niche, selecting a platform and joining relevant affiliate programmes.
  • Creating useful content and building an audience are key to generating affiliate income.

What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a way to earn money by promoting another brand’s products or services. However, you don’t create or sell the product yourself. Instead, you recommend it to an audience through content, links or other methods. When your audience clicks on your affiliate link and completes the process, you get a commission. They could either buy a product, sign up for a service or do what the brand wants done. As a result, you earn a percentage of the sale.
For example, imagine you run a YouTube channel about home technology. On it, you post a review of a laptop and add an affiliate link to an online retailer. If the viewers click on the link and buy the laptop, you earn a percentage of that sale. This is the difference between affiliate and general marketing. You are not simply paid to show an ad. Rather, you earn from the actions of your audience on your referrals.

How Does Affiliate Marketing Work?

The process for affiliate marketing is quite simple. It starts when a brand offers an affiliate programme that you can join. When you join such a programme, you get a unique tracking link that you can share through your content or channels. When a customer clicks on that link, the affiliate programme records the referral. If that same customer completes the action through the link, buys or signs up, the sale is attributed to you as an affiliate. The table below explains these five steps in a simple manner.

1. Who Are the 4 Key Players in Affiliate Marketing?

There are four key players in the process of affiliate marketing: merchant, affiliate, customer and affiliate network. The table below explains each player and their roles.
Although each player has a distinct role, they are connected to one another. In simple terms, you introduce your audience to a brand, while the brand handles the products or services. In return, you get paid a commission for every customer who buys from the brand because of you.

2. How Do Affiliate Links and Tracking Work?

Affiliate links are unique URLs that help a brand identify where a referral came from. When you join an affiliate programme, you receive a unique link or tracking ID. You can then place this link in your content, like product reviews, comparison articles or video descriptions. So, when a customer clicks on the link, their record is stored. Such data is stored in the form of cookies, tracking parameters or other methods. It depends entirely on the programme. After your link is clicked and customers complete the process, the programme connects it to the affiliate who referred them. This leads to the commission that you earn. To make the most of a programme, you should know the exact tracking period and attribution rule. This is crucial as they can vary between brands and programmes. So, you should understand the terms before you begin your journey as an affiliate.

3. How Do Affiliate Marketers Get Paid?

Affiliate marketers are paid when they generate a qualifying action defined by the programme. The payment model of a programme relies on what the brand wants to achieve. There are often three ways by which an affiliate earns.
So, how much can you earn? There is no fixed number, as each programme varies. Some may offer a fixed payment, while others may offer a percentage of the sales. Some brands even offer different commission rates for different products. It is not only the payment that varies, but the schedule as well. Some programmes pay monthly, while others ask the affiliate to reach a minimum threshold to be paid. SaaS affiliate marketers are amongst the highest earners in an affiliate program. A study shows that they earn around $5,967 a month on average.

5% to 25% of the overall sales of major brands come from affiliate marketing.

How to Start Affiliate Marketing: Step by Step

The best thing about affiliate marketing is that you don’t need to build a complicated business from day one. Rather, you can start it from the comfort of your room with your laptop. However, to actually earn from it, you need to know what you have to do. The first step is to choose a niche and find the right audience. Once you have that in sight, create useful content for them around relevant products or services.
The following steps will help you begin your affiliate journey.

1. Choose a Niche

The first step is to find and select the niche you want to work on. A niche is a specific area that you focus on as an affiliate. A niche could be fitness, personal finance, technology, travel, home improvement or any other subject. So, why does this matter?
When you pick a niche, it makes it easier for you to know your audience and create relevant content that answers their needs. Also, it helps you find the best affiliate products that meet your audience’s needs. So, pick a niche that meets your interests, knowledge and your target audience. You should also ensure that there are suitable affiliate programmes available in the niche.

2. Choose Your Platform

The next step is to decide where you will post your content. To pick the best digital platform, you should answer two vital questions:
  1. Which platform matches the type of content you want to create?
  2. Where does your target audience spend most of its time?
The answers to these two questions will narrow down the platforms you need to focus on. For example, you can opt for a website or blog for detailed reviews, comparisons and guides. You can also opt for YouTube to visually review and use a product. Social media platforms are another option to share shorter recommendations and build an audience. A mistake that you should avoid is jumping on every platform. This will confuse you and your audience. Rather, pick one suitable channel and begin from there. This will make it easier for you to create consistent content and understand what works.

3. Find the Affiliate Programmes

After you have picked your niche and platform, the next step is to find affiliate programmes that match your audience. So, how do you find these programmes? What you can do is either search for brands that offer affiliate programmes directly, or find offers through affiliate networks and marketplaces.
Once you find these programmes, take your time to review them in detail. You should check their commission structure, payment terms, product relevance and restrictions, if any. Don’t make the mistake of going after high commission. The best choice isn’t always the one that pays the most. Instead, find a programme that is useful and relevant to your audience.

4. Create Helpful Content

Once you have completed your research, it is time to act on it. For that, you need to create useful and relevant content for your audience. This plays a crucial role in a successful digital marketing strategy. So, how can you make useful content? The one thing you need to be clear on is that your content should help people make informed decisions. For this, you could write product reviews, create comparisons, publish guides, answer common questions or show how a product works. All this is better than just adding affiliate links to your page.
Your content should add value beyond what the brand is already giving. This includes honest explanations of the product or service, practical advice and first-hand experience. These help make your content genuinely useful for the audience. As a result, you gain the trust of your audience and traffic to your channels.

5. Drive Traffic to Your Content

Even if you make useful content, that does not guarantee success. You also need to ensure that people can find it. To drive traffic to your content, make use of the available channels. You can use SEO techniques to appear in search results for relevant searches. You can also use social media and video platforms to reach new audiences. Lastly, email campaigns can help you stay connected with the audience that already follows your content.
The best traffic source relies on your niche, platform and audience. So, instead of using every channel at once, focus on what is most relevant to reach your audience.

6. Track Results and Optimise

These steps will help you start your journey as an affiliate marketer. However, your journey does not end here. You need to keep track of your results and optimise your content to grow more. Once your content starts attracting visitors, monitor how they interact with your affiliate link.
The best way is to check clicks, CTR, conversions, conversion rates and commission earned. This data tells you which content and affiliate offers are performing well. For example, if a product review receives plenty of visitors but very few clicks, you need to check how the affiliate link is presented. If another topic brings regular conversions, you should create more content around it. This practice will help you improve your approach instead of relying on guesswork.

Case Study: Affiliate Marketing Success of Wirecutter

One of the best examples of affiliate marketing is Wirecutter. It is a product recommendation website that began as an independent publication. Its success and growth caught the attention of many, and it was acquired by The New York Times for $30 million in 2016.
The brand built its credibility with the audience by recommending products after thorough research and testing. This made the audience trust its suggestions and buy the products it recommended. Gradually, the brand covered product categories like technology, home goods and travel. To make its content useful, the brand had product comparisons, reviews and guides for its audience, and used the affiliate links the right way. Such practice made Wirecutter a reliable source for the audience, which led to growth in its value as well. In addition, the brand used multiple distribution channels to drive traffic to its website. Their most effective channel was their email campaigns. This allowed them to be more visible and capture a large audience.  
The example of Wirecutter holds a valuable lesson for beginners like you. It teaches you that affiliate marketing is not just about product links that can generate commissions. Rather, the best way is to provide useful information to readers that helps them make better decisions. Hence, the key principle of affiliate marketing is: your content should provide value before it can effectively support monetisation.

Conclusion

Affiliate marketing is a way for you to earn by recommending products or services to an audience. While this method does not require you to make the product yourself, your success depends on how well you present it to your audience. Hence, your success depends on more than just adding an affiliate link to your content.  
To start your journey as an affiliate marketer, pick a niche and a suitable platform that matches it. Then, find relevant affiliate programmes. After this, create useful content with a clear strategy to attract an audience and generate qualifying referrals from them.
If you are just starting your affiliate journey, Expert Affiliates suggest you focus on genuinely helping your audience first. When your recommendations are relevant, useful and credible, affiliate marketing becomes a stable source of online income.
