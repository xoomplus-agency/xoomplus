The Role of AI in Modern Email Marketing

Email marketing has been a key mode of communication between brands and their customers. So much so that nearly 376 billion emails are sent and received worldwide each day. However, modern email campaigns need more than just a single message sent to everyone with the hope it works. Instead, it needs personalised and data-driven content to maximise its results. To do this, brands now rely on AI to make the best use of their email campaigns.  
The role of AI in email marketing has grown greatly. 70% of email marketers expect half of their tasks to be AI-driven by the start of next year. The question is: how? The two models of AI, predictive and generative, help marketers analyse data, create content and optimise campaigns. This lets AI take on repetitive and data-heavy tasks while marketers focus on strategy and creativity.  
This guide explores the role of AI in email marketing. It also explains how AI has changed the way modern email campaigns are planned, created and improved.  

Key Takeaways

  • AI helps email marketers analyse customer data and behaviour to improve their process.
  • AI can segment audiences and personalise email content.
  • Generative AI can create email copy and subject lines faster.
  • Predictive AI can improve send times and predict customer behaviour.
  • AI can test and optimise email campaign performance.

What Is AI in Email Marketing?

AI in email marketing is the use of machine learning to analyse data, find patterns in customer behaviour and improve decisions. In addition, it helps automate tasks that would otherwise take a lot of time.  
Brands now use AI to study purchase patterns, website activity and email engagement of customers to know what they really need and want. This shapes email campaigns that bring the best results for brands. To get the best outcome, modern email campaigns use two distinct AI models: predictive AI and generative AI in email campaign. The former uses data to find patterns and predict customer behaviour. The latter uses information to create new content for the campaign, such as email copy, subject lines and images. Both these models help brands know their audience better and create the right messages for them. This makes AI quite useful at every stage of the email campaign.  
However, the best way is to use AI in email marketing as a support tool. Brands often take AI as a replacement for humans. In reality, that should not be the case. Even with AI, marketers still need to create a clear strategy and review the information. They also ensure that every email campaign reflects the brand identity and provides value to the readers.  
Hence, the right use of AI can do more than just write emails. It can support various parts of the entire email campaign.  
Did you know?
Email marketing generates an average of $36 for every $1 spent, making it one of the highest-returning digital marketing channels.

What Is the Role of AI in Modern Email Marketing?

AI helps email marketers assess customer data to improve the results of their email campaigns. So, how does AI do that? AI helps marketers find patterns in data and gain useful insights from it. Besides audience analysis, AI also helps them create content, test campaigns and analyse performance. Also, AI helps optimise send times to improve engagement. Hence, AI now plays a crucial role at every stage of an email campaign. Here are the key ways marketers use AI to create, manage and improve their email campaigns:  

1. Analyse Customer Data and Behaviour

Email campaigns gather a large amount of data, such as customer lists, email interactions and purchase history. The analysis of such data can take a lot of time if done manually. However, the use of AI has improved and sped up the entire process. AI in email marketing analyses such data quickly to find patterns and useful insights. This includes how customers behave and engage with an email. These insights help marketers make better decisions about their email campaigns.
Did you know?
Around 4.73 billion people use email worldwide, making it a key channel for modern marketing.

2. Segment Email Audiences

Since every customer has distinct needs and interests, sending the same message to everyone can limit a campaign’s effect. This is where AI becomes helpful in email campaigns. It analyses the data and creates groups of customers with similar needs and interests. These groups are then sent content that is more relevant to them. Related: Email Marketing Guide 2025

How AI Turns Testing Into Growth

Email marketing requires testing, but it can be time consuming. AI tools accelerate A/B testing by studying many variations.
For example, an online retailer can segment its audience into frequent and new buyers. Each group can then receive a different type of email that aligns with their behaviour. This helps email marketers segment audiences based on their stage.

‘If Social Media is the cocktail party, then email marketing is the ‘meet up for coffee’. The original 1-to-1 channel.’

Erik Harbison

3. Personalise Email Content

AI takes the idea of groups a step further with customer-centric content. So, what does this mean? AI in email marketing uses data like browsing history, purchase patterns and interests to shape content for each customer. The use of personalised content makes customers feel special and more likely to engage with the email. Such content includes product suggestions, offers, images and subject lines that align with their needs.
Although this can be done manually, it would take a lot of time and effort. AI helps create such email content at a large scale quickly. This lets brands send useful and relevant messages to every customer rapidly.

4. Generate Email Content and Creative Ideas

In 2024, a majority of email marketing teams said it took them two weeks to create a single email. That figure fell to 6% in 2025 with the use of generative AI in email campaigns. Generative AI in email campaigns suggest subject lines, drafts email copy and creates ideas for different audience groups. This speeds up the email creation process.
With AI in email marketing, brands move from a blank page to a workable draft quickly. Marketers then have more time to edit, strategise and finalise content.
Did you know?
61% of users spend 8+ seconds reading a single email, highlighting the importance of engaging email content.

5. Optimise Email Send Times

The timing of an email can affect how likely a customer is to engage with it. This plays a vital role in the success of any email campaign. For a better open rate, AI studies engagement data to predict the best time to send an email. For example, Salesforce uses machine learning to suggest send times based on various factors.
Brands can schedule emails based on each customer’s behaviour instead of a fixed rule. As a result, AI in email marketing helps craft a delivery strategy as well.
Pro Tip:
Emails sent between 10:00 and 11:00 AM are more likely to be opened, making send time an important part of email marketing.

6. Automate Email Campaigns and Customer Journeys

Email marketing involves a series of actions to walk a customer through a funnel. This includes a welcome email, a follow-up message and a reminder based on what they did earlier. AI in email marketing supports these customer journeys through automation. AI helps brands decide the email to send, the time to send it and what to do next. This helps make automated campaigns more responsive to customer behaviour.
For example, a customer who views a product but does not buy may receive a discount. While a new visitor may receive the product catalogue and details in an email. Thus, AI helps brands send out relevant emails to customers based on their stage and journey.

7. AI-Powered A/B Testing

A/B testing lets brands test different ideas in an email campaign to know what works best. However, this can be time-consuming if done manually. This is what AI improves. AI in email marketing lets brands test multiple elements in a single campaign. Such tests include subject lines, email copy, CTA and send times. This lets marketers know which elements work best for which audience. It even helps find versions that perform better based on chosen goals like clicks, conversion and revenue.
AI can make A/B testing more efficient in email marketing by analysing results and identifying patterns across campaigns quickly. As a result, marketers can find the winning version and use it for the rest of the audience.

8. Predicting Customer Behaviour

AI in email marketing helps brands predict what a customer might do next by studying their past behaviour. These predictions help brands decide where to focus their efforts. For example, a customer who shows signs of being inactive may need a re-engagement email. Another customer who is ready to buy may need a relevant offer. This helps brands plan their next move and focus on what customers really want.

9. Analysing and Optimising Campaign Performance

AI in email marketing helps analyse campaign data to find patterns across email engagement, audience response and follow-up actions. It reviews metrics such as clicks and conversions to assess the success of a campaign. This helps brands see which messages get attention and which ones drive results. However, the work of AI does not end with just a report on a campaign. AI is then used to improve the next email marketing strategy as well. This has greatly improved the results of email campaigns. AI tools help marketers to adjust elements like content, timing and target audience based on data. This creates a simple cycle:
Send ➡️ Measure ➡️ Learn ➡️ Improve
Email marketing then becomes a continuous process of testing, learning and optimising.

Case Study: AI-Powered Emails for Success

Boots is a leading health and beauty brand in the UK. Like every brand, Boots wanted to increase sales. To achieve this goal, the brand used customer data to personalise its email campaigns. The brand did this through shopping behaviour, purchase history and customer interest. Their goal was to target the needs of their customers. Based on [Adobe’s Boots case study], this approach helped Boots achieve :
  • 7.7% increase in Click-Through Rate
  • 41.5% open rate from a targeted loyalty email campaign
  • More than 40% of loyalty redemptions resulting in additional purchases
  • Increase in repeat customers
  • Higher member engagement
Boots also used data to optimise email send time, which included abandoned basket reminders and product discount emails. As a result, the brand achieved its goals.
The case study clearly shows that AI for email marketing can lead to better results and build on customers’ trust. With the use of predictive analysis and personalised content, Boots generated visible growth in its sales and engagement.

Conclusion

AI has become a vital part of modern email marketing. It helps brands analyse customer data, personalise content, optimise campaigns and make better decisions. However, AI works best when combined with human judgement, a clear strategy and quality data. Used correctly, it can help marketers make email campaigns more relevant, efficient and effective.
Email marketing experts feel that growth comes through a focused and straightforward system. They know that AI does not replace human planning, but improves its precision through data. Experts help brands adopt AI for email marketing efficiency and help brands engage with their customers more effectively.

FAQs

How does AI improve email marketing?
AI can help marketers make faster, data-driven decisions. It can identify customer patterns, improve personalisation and help optimise campaigns based on real engagement data.
Can AI write email marketing content?

Yes. AI analyses past engagement patterns and suggests subject lines, content variations, and optimal send times. This increases the likelihood that subscribers will open and interact with emails.

How does AI personalise email campaigns?
AI can analyse information such as customer behaviour, preferences and purchase history. It can then help tailor email content to make it more relevant to each recipient.
Can AI optimise email send times?
Yes. AI can analyse previous engagement and identify when individual subscribers are more likely to interact with emails. This can help marketers choose more suitable sending times.
